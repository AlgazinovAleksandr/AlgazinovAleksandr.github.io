My work sits at the intersection of applied AI engineering and research. As an AI Engineer at Sberbank, I design and implement end-to-end multi-agent systems and LLM applications for project management and product analysis automation. Prior to joining the largest bank in Russia, I spent over a year as an AI Researcher at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/About/General_Information.htm)'s [THUIAR](https://thuiar.github.io/) lab, working on robust multimodal intent recognition and multi-agent architectures. With 4.5+ years of ML experience, my applied domains span LLM Agents, NLP, Classical ML, Ranking, Time Series, MLOps, and Applied AI in Finance.

My research has resulted in three publications: [TRACE](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3774905.3795838) *(WWW 2026)*, a transparency-focused reliability scoring framework for web content ([GitHub](https://github.com/AlgazinovAleksandr/TrueGL)); [MATE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19502), a multi-agent system for context-aware modality conversions ([GitHub](https://github.com/AlgazinovAleksandr/Multi-Agent-MATE)); and a [Springer book chapter on AI in network devices](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-19185-4_12).

Before coming to China, I studied Economics at [HSE University](https://www.hse.ru/en/), which is the best university in Russia for Computer Science and Economics. Already at the beginning of my studies, I became interested in Machine Learning. Hence, besides my main course work, I took many courses from the Department of Computer Science, such as Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Big Data, etc.

In addition to working and doing my research, I enjoy sharing my knowledge and experience with others. For instance, I was a speaker at the [Open Data Science conference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v9JS1vsIUkY&list=LL&index=51) (in Russian), where I talked about the methods we used to detect time series anomalies, the challenges we faced, and the approaches we took to overcome them. On the top of that, I run a [telegram channel](https://t.me/krasnorechivyy_ch) (in Russian), where I share my experience in terms of AI, studying, and living in China.
