## Graph-STREAM: RL-Based Traffic Optimization

+ Building a graph-based pipeline to optimize vehicle traffic using reinforcement learning.

+ High traffic load remains a major issue, especially for big cities. Traffic can be forecast and mitigated by changing navigation trajectories, controlling traffic lights, etc. The goal is a unified framework combining anomaly detection, forecasting, and human feedback modeling in traffic systems.

+ [GitHub](https://github.com/AlgazinovAleksandr/STREAM-RL)
